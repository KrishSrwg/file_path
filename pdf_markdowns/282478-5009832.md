## Products Affected:

- Tremfya (guselkumab)

The Plan may authorize coverage of the above products for members meeting the following criteria:

## Covered use: All medically accepted indications unless otherwise excluded

## Moderate to severe plaque psoriasis (Ps)

1. The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
2. The requested medication is prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist; AND
3. ONE of the following:
- a. Involvement of at least 3% of total body surface area prior to the use of the requested medications; OR
- b. Hands, feet, scalp, face, or genital area affected; AND
4. ONE of the following:
- a. An inadequate response or adverse reaction to one biologic DMARD that is FDA-approved for plaque psoriasis; OR
- b. An inadequate response or adverse reaction to at least a 3 consecutive month trial of any one of the following combinations (please note: these combinations DO NOT have to be used concurrently)
- ii. one topical agent plus one phototherapy; OR
- i. one topical agent plus one systemic agent; OR
- iii. one systemic agent plus one phototherapy; OR
- iv. two systemic agents; OR
- c. A contraindication to methotrexate, as determined by the prescriber; AND
5. Documentation that the requested dose will not exceed FDA-approved dosing (see Appendix A).

## Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA)

1. The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
2. The requested medication is prescribed by or in consultation with a rheumatologist; AND
3. ONE of the following:
- d. An inadequate response or adverse reaction to at least a 3 consecutive month trial of one traditional disease modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) or contraindication to all traditional DMARDs; OR
- e. An inadequate response or adverse reaction to one biologic DMARD that is FDA-approved for psoriatic arthritis; AND
4. Documentation that the requested dose will not exceed FDA-approved dosing (see Appendix A).

## Moderately to Severely Active Ulcerative Colitis (UC)

1. The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
2. One of the following:
- a. Member has tried one systemic therapy (such as 6- mercaptopurine, azathioprine, cyclosporine, tacrolimus, a corticosteroid such as prednisone or methylprednisolone, or a previous trial of a biologic); OR

- b. Member has pouchitis and has tried an antibiotic (such as metronidazole or ciprofloxacin), probiotic, corticosteroid enema, or mesalamine enema; AND
3. The requested medication is prescribed by or in consultation with a gastroenterologist; AND
4. Documentation that the requested dose will not exceed FDA-approved dosing (see Appendix A)

## Moderate to severely active Crohn's disease (CD)

1. The member is 18 years of age or older; AND
2. The medication is prescribed by or in consultation with a gastroenterologist; AND
3. One of the following:
- a. a. Member has tried or is currently taking corticosteroids, or corticosteroids are contraindicated in this member; OR
- b. b. Member has tried one other conventional systemic therapy for Crohn's disease (such as azathioprine, 6- mercaptopurine, methotrexate, or a previous trial of a biologic; Note: a trial of mesalamine does not count); OR
- c. Member has enterocutaneous (perianal or abdominal) or rectovaginal fistulas; OR
- d. Member had ileocolonic resection (to reduce the chance of Crohn's disease recurrence); AND
4. Documentation that the requested dose will not exceed FDA-approved dosing (see Appendix A)

## the requested dose will not exceed FDA-approved dosing (see Appendix A).

## Reauthorization criteria

1. Initial criteria has been met; AND
2. One of the following:
- a. The member has had a positive response to therapy; OR
- b. Documentation of clinical justification to continue therapy with the requested medication

## Coverage Duration

Initial: 6 months

Reauthorization: 12 months
