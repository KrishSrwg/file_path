## Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA)

- Diagnosis of active psoriatic arthritis with one of the following:
- o Actively inflamed joints
- o Dactylitis
- o Enthesitis
- o Axial disease
- o Active skin and/or nail involvement
- Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to TWO of the following:
- o Cimzia (certolizumab pegol)
- o Humira (adalimumab)
- o Simponi (golimumab)
- o Stelara (ustekinumab)
- o Tremfya (guselkumab)
- o Skyrizi (risankizumab-rzaa)
- o Rinvoq (upadacitinib)
- o Xeljanz/XR (tofacitinib/ER).

## Prophylaxis for Acute Graft versus Host Disease (aGVHD)

- Used for prophylaxis of acute graft versus host disease (aGVHD)
- Patient will receive hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT) from a matched or 1 allele-mismatched unrelated donor
- Recommended antiviral prophylactic treatment for Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV) reactivation (e.g., acyclovir) will be administered prior to Orencia
- Continued for six months after HSCT
- Used in combination with both of the following:
- o calcineurin inhibitor (e.g., cyclosporine, tacrolimus)
- o methotrexate

## Ankylosing spondylitis (AS)

- Diagnosis of active ankylosing spondylitis
- Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to two NSAIDs (e.g., diclofenac, ibuprofen, indomethacin, meloxicam, naproxen)

## Crohn's disease (CD)

- Diagnosis of moderately to severely active Crohn's disease
- One of the following:
- o frequent diarrhea and abdominal pain,
- o at least 10% weight loss
- o complications such as obstruction, fever, abdominal mass
- o abnormal lab values (e.g. C-reactive protein)
- o CD Activity Index greater than 220
- Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to one of the following conventional therapies
- o 6-mercaptopurine
- o Azathioprine
- o corticosteroids (e.g., prednisone, methylprednisolone)
- o methotrexate

## Ulcerative Colitis (UC)

- Diagnosis of moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis
- One of the following
- o Greater than 6 stools per day
- o frequent blood in the stools
- o frequent urgency
- o presence of ulcers
- o abnormal lab values (e.g. hemoglobin, ESR, CRP)
- o dependent on, or refractory to, corticosteroids
- Trial and failure, contraindication, or intolerance to one of the following conventional therapies
- o 6-mercaptopurine
- o aminosalicylate [e.g., mesalamine sulfasalazine, azathioprine, Corticosteroids (e.g., prednisone, methylprednisolone).
