- A. Atopic Dermatitis (must meet all):
1. Diagnosis of atopic dermatitis affecting one of the following (a or b):
- a. At least 10% of the member's body surface area (BSA);

3. Prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist or allergist;
4. Age ≥ 12 years;
5. Failure of both of the following (a and b), unless contraindicated or clinically significant adverse effects are experienced:
- a. One formulary medium to very high potency topical corticosteroid used for ≥ 2 weeks;
